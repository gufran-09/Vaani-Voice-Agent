# Menu Guidelines & Categories

Cafe Vaani features fresh, house-made items organized into primary categories:

1. **Beverages — Hot & Cold**:
   - Signature South Indian Filter Coffee (traditional brass dabarah style)
   - Cold Brew Coffee (slow steeped for 16 hours)
   - Masala Chai (brewed with fresh cardamom and ginger)
   - Espresso, Cappuccino, Cafe Latte, Flat White
2. **Bakery & Quick Bites**:
   - Fresh Bun Maska (with Iranian-style butter spread)
   - Butter Croissants & Almond Croissants
   - Vegetable Samosa (crispy pastry stuffed with spiced potatoes and peas)
   - Banana Walnut Cake
3. **Breakfast & South Indian Specials**:
   - Rava Idli with ghee & coconut chutney
   - Crispy Medu Vada
   - Masala Dosa
