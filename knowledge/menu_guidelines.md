# Menu Guidelines & Categories

Cafe Vaani features fresh, house-made items organized into primary categories across all branches:

1. **Hot Beverages**:
   - South Indian Filter Coffee (traditional brass dabarah style, freshly brewed chicory blend with frothy milk)
   - Cutting Masala Chai (brewed with fresh cardamom, cloves, and ginger)
   - Classic Espresso, Cappuccino, and Masala Chai Latte
   - Rich Hot Chocolate

2. **Cold Beverages**:
   - Cold Brew Coffee (slow steeped for 18 hours, smooth and low in acidity over ice)
   - Classic Cold Coffee (creamy blended chilled milk coffee with vanilla bean)
   - Fresh Alphonso Mango Lassi
   - Watermelon Mint Cooler

3. **Hot Snacks & Savories**:
   - Golden Crispy Samosas (stuffed with spiced potatoes and peas)
   - Mumbai Masala Vada Pav (spicy potato patty in soft pav with garlic chutney)
   - Spiced Veg Puff (flaky baked puff pastry filled with curried veggies)
   - Paneer Roll & Tandoori Paneer Tikka Wrap
   - Crispy Medu Vada (fried lentil doughnuts with coconut chutney)
   - Cheese Garlic Bread & Crispy Corn Chaat

4. **Tiffin & Breakfast**:
   - Fresh Irani Bun Maska (warm bun with rich butter slab)
   - Tawa Masala Dosa (crispy fermented crepe with potato mash)
   - Artisanal Avocado Toast on sourdough

5. **Bakery & Desserts**:
   - Fresh Butter Croissant & Double Chocolate Muffin
   - Warm Chocolate Walnut Brownie
   - Gulab Jamun in rose-saffron syrup
   - Silky Mango Panna Cotta

6. **Main Course**:
   - Dal Makhani Bowl with jeera rice
   - Creamy Garlic Mushroom Pasta

**Rule**: Always quote prices and availability dynamically from PostgreSQL database query tools (`search_menu`, `check_availability`). Never invent prices.
