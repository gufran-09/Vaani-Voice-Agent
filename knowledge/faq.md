# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: Do you have Wi-Fi?**
  - **A**: Yes, we have high-speed guest Wi-Fi. Network name is `CafeVaani_Guest` with password `filtercoffee`.
- **Q: Is parking available?**
  - **A**: Dedicated valet parking is available in front of the cafe on 100 Feet Road, along with two-wheeler parking.
- **Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
  - **A**: We accept all UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, and cash upon pickup.
- **Q: Do you offer vegan milk substitutes?**
  - **A**: Yes, oat milk and almond milk are available for all coffees on request.
- **Q: Can I pre-order for pickup?**
  - **A**: Absolutely! Once confirmed over this call, your order will be hot and ready at the takeaway counter by the stated ETA.
