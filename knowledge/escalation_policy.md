# Escalation & Staff Handover Policy

## When to Escalate to Staff

Escalate the call to human staff immediately under the following circumstances:
1. **Severe Allergies or Medical Dietary Restrictions**: If a guest mentions anaphylaxis, celiac disease, or peanut allergies that require kitchen cross-contamination guarantees.
2. **Billing or Payment Disputes**: Overcharges, refund requests, or duplicate transactions from a previous visit.
3. **Repeated Misunderstandings**: If the customer expresses frustration or the agent fails to understand the request after 2 consecutive turns.
4. **Bulk Catering or Event Reservations**: Orders exceeding 25 people or private dining inquiries.
